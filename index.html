How to play
  • Builders gather wood, stone and food, then spend it to finish all 5 buildings.
  • Saboteurs (1–2, secret) damage buildings, steal from the storehouse and cause crises. They fake work so nobody notices.
  • Crises: Fire and Power surge must be fixed by holding E before the timer ends, or the saboteurs win. Lights out shrinks your vision.
  • Inspect sites (F) for clues: boot prints, who was nearby, ledger entries. Report sabotage (R) or ring the bell to hold a meeting.
  • In meetings, talk, share clues, then vote someone out. Builders win by finishing the settlement or exposing every saboteur.
