Build & Betray

Build a settlement together. One of you is secretly tearing it down.

or join friends

How to play
  • Each round, everyone secretly picks a location. Builders gather 🪵🪨🌾, build at the 🔨 Workshop, or audit at the 📦 Storehouse.
  • Saboteurs (secret) pick a location too, plus a secret move: break a place, steal goods, or start a fire (all tasks stop until builders put it out together).
  • Broken places must be visited to be repaired, and they drain settlement health every round.
  • After each round there is a meeting: you only know who you saw at your own location. Compare notes, then vote someone out.
  • Builders win: finish the settlement, or vote out every saboteur.
  • Saboteurs win: health hits 0, a fire burns 2 rounds, 15 rounds pass, or they equal the builders.
