Build & Betray

Among Us, with no map. Finish your tasks. One of you is secretly killing.

or join friends

How to play
  • You travel between locations (takes a few seconds) and see who is there.
  • Every builder gets their own list of tasks, each at a specific location with its own minigame. Finish all builders' tasks (meter 100%) to win. You can't move while working.
  • Saboteurs get a fake task list. They can pretend to work, kill a builder in the same location (then they're locked there for a few seconds), or sabotage: break a place or start a fire that halts all tasks.
  • Bodies stay where they fell. Anyone in that location can report it. You can also ring the emergency bell at the 📣 Plaza (once each).
  • Meetings: discuss, share limited clues from your notebook, then vote someone out.
  • Meter check: when someone finishes a task, the meter should rise. If it doesn't...
