Build & Betray

Finish the tasks together. One of you is secretly killing and sabotaging.

or join friends

How to play
  • Each round, everyone secretly picks a location. Once you pick, you are stuck there doing that place's minigame. You cannot move while working.
  • Every finished task fills the task meter. At 100% the builders win.
  • Saboteurs (secret) pick a location too, plus a move: kill a builder at that location, break the place (tasks there stop until someone repairs it), or start a fire (all tasks stop until builders put it out together).
  • After every round there is a meeting: bodies are announced, but you only know who you saw at your own location. Compare notes, then vote someone out.
  • Saboteurs win if they equal the builders, a fire burns for 2 rounds, or 12 rounds pass.
